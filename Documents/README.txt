TECHNOVA ENTERPRISE KNOWLEDGE DATASET
======================================

Fictional enterprise documents created for the
AI Document Intelligence & Enterprise Knowledge Assistant portfolio project.

Documents:
- Employee Handbook
- Leave and Time-Off Policy
- IT Acceptable Use & Information Security Policy
- Employee Benefits & Reimbursement Guide
- Employee Onboarding & Internal Operations Guide
- HR & Workplace Procedures Manual

These documents are intentionally detailed and cross-referenced so the RAG
pipeline can retrieve information from multiple documents.

Example questions:
- How many casual leaves are available per year?
- How many sick leaves are available?
- How many earned leaves can be carried forward?
- How early should planned leave be requested?
- Can I use personal cloud storage for company files?
- What should I do if I click a suspicious link?
- What happens during the first 30 days of onboarding?
- Is probation automatically completed after six months?
- Who can access employee records?
- Can a manager create a different leave entitlement?

IMPORTANT:
These are fictional portfolio documents. They are not real TechNova policies,
legal advice, or employment contracts.
